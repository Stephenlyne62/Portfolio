RestBoo

Role: Lead Front-End Engineer

RestBoo is a large-scale restaurant booking and management platform built for customers, restaurant partners, staff, and administrators. I built and owned the complete front-end experience, working within a multi-developer team while taking responsibility for the user-facing side of the application.

The platform includes restaurant discovery, booking flows, profile management, dashboards, messaging, image uploads, map-based features, authentication, and role-based interfaces. My focus was on creating clear user journeys across a complex multi-role product.

  • Built customer, restaurant partner, staff, and administrator interfaces
  • Implemented booking, messaging, profile, dashboard, and administration workflows
  • Integrated REST APIs, authentication, image uploads, and map-based functionality
  • Designed responsive, role-based user experiences across the platform

World Cup Predictor

Role: Designer & Developer

World Cup Predictor is a tournament prediction platform I designed, built, and deployed to replace a manual spreadsheet and group chat process for a private football competition.

Users can submit fixture predictions, make outright tournament selections, track scores, and compete through automated leaderboards. The project turned a messy manual process into a dedicated web application with admin tools, scoring logic, and dynamic rankings.

Movie Match

Role: Designer & Developer

Movie Match is a mood-based movie discovery platform designed to reduce decision fatigue. Instead of forcing users to scroll through large streaming catalogues, the application helps people choose films based on how they feel.

The current version is a live MVP that demonstrates the core experience: mood-based recommendations, archive browsing, search, filtering, detailed movie pages, trailers, and a persistent watchlist. The longer-term product vision is a curated weekly recommendation service, with future expansion into music recommendations and short-form social content to build an engaged discovery community.

Oscillator Synthesiser Research

Role: Researcher & Developer

My MSc thesis project explores audio synthesis and digital signal processing through a comparative study of software synthesiser architectures in C++.

The project involves developing two oscillator-based synthesisers, designing an A/B testing process, and evaluating the systems through technical analysis and user feedback.
