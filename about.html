Background

From music
technology to
software

My route into software development began with music. I originally studied Music Production, where working with recording technology and digital audio developed my interest in the technical side of creative tools.

I later moved into computing and web development, building experience across JavaScript, TypeScript, React, databases, APIs, and full-stack applications. What initially started as an interest in building for the web developed into a broader interest in software engineering and product development.

Audio Software

Bringing both sides together

My MSc thesis brought my software and music backgrounds together through C++ audio programming and digital signal processing. I developed an educational software synthesizer and explored how interface design could make sound design more accessible to novice users.

The project combined software engineering, DSP, human-computer interaction, and user evaluation, and the thesis received a distinction.

Development

Building software around people

I'm particularly interested in front-end engineering because it sits at the point where technical decisions directly affect the person using the product. I care about responsive interfaces, accessibility, maintainable code, and making complex functionality feel straightforward.

At the same time, I'm comfortable working beyond the interface. My projects have involved authentication, databases, REST APIs, server-side logic, testing, and deployment, giving me an understanding of how the wider application fits together.
