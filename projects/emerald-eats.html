The Project

A digital home for
a growing food business.

Emerald Eats needed a modern website capable of presenting menus, catering services, merchandise, promotional media and business contact information through a clear, responsive experience.

The site also needed to evolve alongside the business, allowing content, imagery, menus and branding to change without requiring a complete redesign.

My Role

From requirements
to production.

I worked directly with the client to translate business requirements into a complete production website, taking responsibility for implementation, responsive behaviour, deployment and subsequent updates.

  • Responsive multi-section website
  • Indoor and outdoor menu presentation
  • Galleries and promotional media
  • Merchandise presentation
  • Mobile and performance improvements
  • Post-launch maintenance and client updates

Challenges

Working with a real client

Unlike a fixed academic brief, the website continued to change as the client's business, content and priorities developed.

01

Changing requirements

The website evolved after launch with new branding, menu changes, content updates and design revisions.

02

Responsive layouts

Large imagery, menus and promotional sections needed to remain clear and consistent across desktop and mobile devices.

03

Media performance

Image and video-heavy sections required attention to loading behaviour and performance without compromising the visual presentation.

04

Client communication

Feedback and business requirements had to be translated into practical technical changes throughout the project's lifecycle.

Outcome

What Emerald Eats demonstrates

Delivery and ongoing ownership of a real commercial website — from client requirements and responsive implementation through production launch and post-launch updates.

The project demonstrates my ability to work directly with a client, adapt to changing requirements and balance technical decisions with the practical needs of a live business.
